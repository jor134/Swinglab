What this can and cannot measure

It tracks your body only. There is no club tracking, so no club path, shaft plane, face angle or impact position. The target ranges come from common coaching guidance, not from a validated dataset, and a single 2D camera cannot resolve depth — treat every number as an estimate with a few degrees of noise. Impact is detected as the frame of peak hand speed, which is close to but not exactly impact.
